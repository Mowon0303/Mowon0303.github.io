I study how language models behave inside real decision systems: where they add value, where they fall short, and how to measure the difference.

I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Southern California in May 2026 and work as a Research Assistant in USC's SIAS Lab. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of California, Davis.

Research interests

  • LLM agent evaluation: separating what a model contributes from what the surrounding system contributes.
  • Reliable AI systems: tool-first workflows, evidence citation, and confirmation-gated actions.
  • Human-in-the-loop machine learning: directing human review to the outputs most likely to be wrong.
